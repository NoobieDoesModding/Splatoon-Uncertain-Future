# Lore:

Somewhere in Inkopolis plaza, 3 years after Mr.Grizz was defeated by Neo Agent 3, a new threat was looming over the stability of the splatoon world.

### Backstory

In Octo Valley, where the squidbeak splatoon thought was abandonned and useless was DJ Octavio however he wasn't there for peace, he was there to prepare a future war, he would train his troop harshly, most of them were thrown out into the ruins of the deepsea metro. (it was destroyed by Agent 3 soon after splatoon 3 happened)
At the same time, a curious octoling, living in the new united (kinda) world, where inklings and octolings lived as brothers and sisters. Our protagonist isn't smart, but knows (hopefully) how to fight since they deserted the Octarian military to find rest for them and their family. Sensing danger, they immediatly get to Octo Valley, where they're greeted with Craig Cuttlefish, he gives them some octarian gear such as the octoshot, the Octoling scope and boots. Our protagonist (Goated btw) is now ready to avoid the Great II Turf War.

# Difficulty levels:


### Is the mod really this easy?

The mod won't be easy, this might be the same level layout, yet, but it's gonna take a lot of shots to successfully splat an octarian.
The weapon you're using is an improved octoshot made by Sheldon, The precision isn't at it's finest, nor it's ink consumption, but it's damage is monstruous, it can splat a regular inkling/octoling in 2 shots. C.Cuttlefish made sure you can properly test this weapon in Octo Valley, providing you several different dummies, as well as a basketball hoop floating on top of the boss ketle.
Since E.Octavio brutally trained his troops, your weapon's efficiency is greatly compromised. You'll rely on sheer luck if you want to splat an enemy without emptying your whole ink tank.
